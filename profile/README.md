# Flintlamp

Business software that tells you what matters.

Flintlamp builds business software that thinks like an owner. Most tools collect data; ours tell you what matters: what each job really earns, which customer needs a call, where the money goes. Every number exact, every screen calm and simple, on phone and desktop alike. ERP and CRM share one view of the business, and the family keeps growing, so a company never outgrows us. Premium, private by design, built in the UAE for businesses everywhere.

Public demos are available without sign-up. Early access is free by request.
